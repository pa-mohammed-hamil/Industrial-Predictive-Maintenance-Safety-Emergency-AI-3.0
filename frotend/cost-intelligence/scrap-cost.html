Quality Cost Optimization Insights

Automated observations from synthetic production data

Quality cost methodology: All production records are synthetic. Scrapped units are treated as permanently rejected units, while reworked units are a separate category. Material loss is valued using manufacturing material cost, not selling price. Recovery value is deducted as a credit. The scenario assumes scrap reduction proportionally reduces material loss, disposal expense and scrap recovery. It assumes no change in production volume or product mix. Rework and scrap populations must remain mutually exclusive. Do not double count these costs in production-loss, downtime-cost, labor-cost or other financial modules. Actual savings require verified production quality records, scrap disposition, recovery receipts and implementation costs.
