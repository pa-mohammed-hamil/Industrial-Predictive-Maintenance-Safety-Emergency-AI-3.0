Descriptive findings from the selected financial scenario

Forecast methodology: This is a deterministic scenario model using synthetic equipment and financial data. Failure-related costs are probability-weighted expected losses, not guaranteed expenditures. The model assumes at most one modeled failure per asset over the forecast horizon. Repair and replacement are alternative failure outcomes. Emergency costs are separately modeled to avoid intentionally double counting production and downtime losses. The sensitivity bands are not statistical confidence intervals. Production deployment requires validated cost histories, equipment reliability models, tariff data, financial reconciliation, uncertainty calibration and engineering review.
