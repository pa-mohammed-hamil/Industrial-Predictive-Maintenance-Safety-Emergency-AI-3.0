Forecasting notice: This dashboard uses synthetic maintenance cost assumptions, illustrative annual failure probabilities and deterministic expected-value calculations. Forecast bands are scenario sensitivity ranges, not validated statistical confidence intervals. No trained forecasting model or production finance data is used. Costs are modeled as non-overlapping categories, and annual failure risk is approximated with a constant hazard rate. Production use requires verified maintenance records, cost accounting, model backtesting, calibration, uncertainty analysis and finance review.
