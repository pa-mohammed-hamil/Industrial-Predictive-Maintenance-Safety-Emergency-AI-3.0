Financial and operational notice: All downtime records, durations, loss rates and cost estimates are synthetic. Production loss is modeled as lost contribution margin rather than gross sales revenue. Labor costs represent incremental idle labor expense and recovery costs represent separate restart-related expenditure, avoiding intentional double-counting. Scenario-based avoided cost is hypothetical and is not verified savings. This dashboard is not connected to live PLC, SCADA, MES or financial systems and must not be used for safety-critical operational decisions.
