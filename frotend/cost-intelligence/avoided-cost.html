Financial modeling notice: All asset probabilities, downtime durations, cost inputs and maintenance effectiveness assumptions are illustrative. Avoided cost represents modeled expected losses reduced, not verified savings or a confirmed prevented failure. This model assumes one potential failure event per asset over the annual reference period and scales event probability to shorter horizons using a constant-hazard assumption. The model excludes taxes, discounting, shared-cause failures, uncertainty intervals and overlapping loss categories. Do not use these estimates for investment approval without validated historical failure data, finance-reviewed costs and engineering review.
