Inventory accounting notice: All data are synthetic. Inventory value is a stock balance, forecast consumption is a projected expense, and replenishment is a purchase commitment; these figures should not be added together as a single total operating cost. Carrying cost is an estimated inventory holding expense and is not included in forecast consumption. The reorder model uses a 30-day planning month, constant daily demand and configurable safety stock. It excludes open purchase orders, reserved stock, minimum order quantities, obsolescence, supplier constraints, taxes, freight, currency movements and actual lead-time variability. Reconcile issued spare-part expenses with repair-cost and maintenance-cost modules to prevent double counting.
