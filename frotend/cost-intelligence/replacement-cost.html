Financial modeling notice: All equipment and prices are synthetic. Each asset represents one hypothetical replacement, not an expected number of replacements. Direct replacement cost includes purchase, installation, commissioning, logistics, decommissioning, disposal and salvage recovery. Downtime uses contribution margin rather than gross revenue. Future lifecycle NPC includes recurring maintenance only; it excludes future failures, taxes, financing, terminal salvage and additional replacement cycles. The repair comparison is an immediate intervention comparison, not a full lifecycle decision. Avoid adding downtime or labor components again when consolidating this dashboard with other cost modules.
