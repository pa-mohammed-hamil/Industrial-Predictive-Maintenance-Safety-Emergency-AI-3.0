ROI methodology: This dashboard uses synthetic asset costs and failure probabilities. Failure benefits are estimated as the difference between baseline and intervention event probabilities under a constant-hazard, first-failure model. Repair and replacement are mutually exclusive outcomes. Recurring monitoring expenses and initial capital are included in project costs. ROI is calculated from undiscounted net benefits; NPV uses discounted monthly incremental cash flows. Payback is calculated from cumulative undiscounted cash flow. These estimates exclude taxes, financing, depreciation, salvage value and secondary failures. All decisions require validated reliability data, engineering assessment and financial review.
