Repair Cost Intelligence Insights

Repair cost methodology: All asset data and repair expenses are synthetic. The selected period scales modeled annual repair activity, rather than retrieving actual historical work orders. Labor, spare parts, contractor services and logistics are separate direct-cost categories. Production downtime loss is shown separately and must not be added again when consolidating with the production-loss or downtime-cost dashboards. Repair-versus- replacement results compare single interventions only; they do not account for future reliability, residual asset life, warranty, financing, tax or discounted life-cycle costs. Validate all estimates with CMMS work orders, supplier quotes, production records and finance-approved cost models.
