Financial modeling notice: All production data are synthetic. Lost units represent equivalent output loss from distinct causes: equipment downtime, reduced throughput during operating hours, defective units and startup scrap. Financial impact is estimated using unit contribution margin rather than gross sales revenue. The model assumes losses can be valued independently and does not include demand limitations, inventory buffers, production recovery capacity, contractual penalties or additional repair and labor costs. Do not add these values to another downtime-cost dashboard without reconciling overlapping loss categories.
