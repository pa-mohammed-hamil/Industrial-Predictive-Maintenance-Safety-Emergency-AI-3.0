Transparent financial observations and decision cautions

Decision-model limitations: All data are synthetic. This model assumes both alternatives deliver equivalent production service throughout the selected horizon. Expected future failure costs are calculated using annual event rates and do not represent validated survival predictions. Remaining useful life is displayed as an engineering warning signal, not as a hard automatic replacement deadline. Neither tax effects, financing, salvage values, major scheduled overhauls nor replacement lead-time uncertainty are included. A financially cheaper option is not necessarily safe or technically feasible. Validate reliability assumptions, safety constraints, repair feasibility, supplier quotations and lifecycle costs before making operational decisions.
