Replacement Planning & Lifecycle Insights

Lifecycle methodology: All equipment records and costs are synthetic. Age is calculated from installation dates using the current browser date. Lifecycle stages are rule-based planning labels, not actual work-order states. Remaining useful life, health and failure probabilities are illustrative estimates. TCO includes acquisition reference cost, maintenance, energy, routine downtime and expected failure consequences, without discounting. For existing assets, historical acquisition cost is not a future cash outflow and should not be included in incremental repair-versus-replacement decisions. Future milestones are planning estimates, not approved maintenance schedules.
