Maintenance & Inspection Recommendations

Demonstration methodology: All machine records, sensor values, health scores, remaining useful life estimates, annual failure probabilities and component condition scores are synthetic. The refresh action changes simulated sensor values, not live equipment measurements. Health categories use fixed demonstration thresholds: Healthy ≥80, Warning 65–79 and Critical below 65. Sensor limits and component scores are not validated for any particular machine. Real-world safety or maintenance decisions require calibrated sensors, validated predictive models, maintenance history and qualified engineering review.
