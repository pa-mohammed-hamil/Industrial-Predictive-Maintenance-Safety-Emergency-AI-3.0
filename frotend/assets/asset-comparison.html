Comparison methodology: All equipment data are synthetic. Acquisition cost is included as a new-asset lifecycle reference and is not necessarily a future cash outflow for already-owned equipment. Maintenance includes routine service and spare-part expenditure, while downtime loss and expected failure consequences are modeled as separate, non-overlapping cost categories. The expected failure component excludes downtime already represented in the downtime category. Failure probabilities are annual assumptions, and expected failure cost is calculated using a linear annual expectation. The illustrative TCO is undiscounted and does not include financing, tax, inflation, disposal or salvage. Equipment health and remaining useful life are illustrative rather than outputs from a trained predictive maintenance model.
