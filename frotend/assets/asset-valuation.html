Asset Valuation Intelligence

Financial methodology and limitations: All equipment records are synthetic. Straight-line book values are modeled from acquisition cost, useful life and residual value, assuming acquisition at the beginning of the installation year. Market value is an illustrative condition-adjusted resale estimate, not an independent appraisal. Replacement cost is modeled using annual inflation. Net realizable value deducts estimated disposal costs from modeled market value. The displayed value shortfall is a screening indicator only, not a recognized accounting impairment loss. Formal impairment testing requires applicable accounting standards, recoverable amount calculations and professional review. These figures are not suitable for audited financial reporting, insurance coverage or lending decisions.
